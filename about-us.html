Our process

We keep every flooring project organized so you know what to expect from the first conversation through the final walkthrough.

We start by understanding your space, goals, budget, and timeline. Then we help you choose the right flooring option, whether that means hardwood, laminate, LVP, carpet, tile, or sheet vinyl.
You get a straightforward quote and realistic expectations before work begins. We explain what makes sense for your project, including when a simpler option is the smarter investment.
Once approved, we complete the work with attention to labor quality and clean execution. That includes new flooring installation, hardwood sand and finish, tile work, or flooring removal.
We finish with a walkthrough to make sure the details are right and the result matches the plan. CFS Flooring proudly serves Lake Oswego, West Linn, and Wilsonville with responsive, local service.
End result
A clear plan, quality workmanship, and a finished floor that fits your space and goals.
